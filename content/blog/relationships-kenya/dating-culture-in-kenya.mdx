# Dating Culture in Kenya: What Has Changed

Dating culture in Kenya has changed significantly over the last decade. Technology, social media, urbanization, and changing lifestyles have influenced how people meet and build relationships.

## Traditional Dating

Traditionally, relationships often developed through family, friends, schools, workplaces, and local communities.

## Modern Dating

Today, many people connect through online platforms and social networks before meeting in person.

## Key Changes

- Increased use of smartphones
- Greater independence among young adults
- More online communication
- Expanding social circles

## Conclusion

Dating culture continues to evolve as Kenya becomes increasingly connected.

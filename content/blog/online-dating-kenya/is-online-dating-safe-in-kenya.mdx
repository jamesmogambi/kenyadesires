# Is Online Dating Safe in Kenya?

Online dating can be a positive experience when approached carefully.

## Common Challenges

Some users may encounter:

- Fake profiles
- Misleading information
- Privacy concerns

## How to Stay Safe

Follow these simple guidelines:

- Protect personal information
- Verify profiles when possible
- Meet in public places
- Inform a trusted friend if meeting someone

Trust develops over time. It is important to communicate openly and avoid rushing important decisions.
